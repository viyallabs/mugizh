## 1. What this notice covers

This notice explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It covers the app, the website and our WhatsApp channel.

It follows the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules 2025 (DPDP). Until those duties start (about 13 May 2027), it also follows the Information Technology Act 2000, section 43A, and the SPDI Rules 2011, which treat health information as sensitive personal data. **[VALIDATE: dates and wording with lawyer; C01, N1]**

## 2. Who can use the app

You must be **18 or older**. In this version you cannot create a profile for a child, and you cannot book for someone else. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete its data, except what the law makes us keep (section 8). Family profiles will come in a later version, with a guardian's verified consent. **[Founder decision 3, 14 Nov 2026; C04]**

## 4. Your consent

- We ask for each purpose separately. Nothing is ticked for you.
- **Needed to use the app:** P-ACCOUNT, P-CARE, P-RECORD and P-REMIND. Without them we cannot book or run your care, so the app cannot work. You can still withdraw them at any time by closing your account (section 7).
- **Your choice:** P-WHATSAPP, P-MEDNAMES, P-ANALYTICS and P-NEWS. Saying no does not change the care you get.
- **Sharing your history with a doctor** needs a separate permission each time, for one consultation and a set time (P-SHARE). You can end it at any time and the doctor loses access straight away.
- You can withdraw any consent as easily as you gave it, in **Settings → Privacy**. Withdrawing does not undo what was lawfully done before. **[C01, K-DP-02]**

## 7. Your rights

You can:

1. **See your data:** get a summary of what we hold and who we shared it with.
2. **Correct or update it:** fix wrong or incomplete details. Clinical notes are corrected by your doctor as a new version; the old version is kept (K-CS-05).
3. **Delete it:** close your account and erase your data. Some records must be kept for a legal period (section 8); we lock those away and delete the rest.
4. **Withdraw consent:** at any time, for any purpose.
5. **Name a nominee:** someone who can use your rights if you die or cannot act yourself.
6. **Complain:** to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

Use **Settings → Privacy → My data rights**, or write to the Grievance Officer. How we handle requests and how long we take is in the data-rights process. **[C02]**

## 9. How we protect it

- Encryption in transit and at rest; extra encryption for health information.
- Apart from the doctor you are seeing, and each doctor's own notes about you, doctors see your history only with your permission. Every look is recorded, and you can see the log.
- Our staff can open records only for a stated reason, for a short time, and every access is recorded.
- Reminders on your lock screen never show a condition; medicine names appear only if you choose (P-MEDNAMES).
- If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires. **[C03, K-DP-03, K-DP-09, K-SEC-08]**
