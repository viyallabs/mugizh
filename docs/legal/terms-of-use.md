## 2. What we do and do not do

- We run a platform that lists **doctors we have verified** (registration checked with State Medical Councils or the NMC), and lets you book and pay for appointments and online consultations with them.
- **Doctors are independent.** Each doctor decides on your diagnosis and treatment and is responsible for it. We do not employ doctors to treat you, and we do not influence their clinical decisions.
- **Search results are never paid for.** How results are ordered is explained in plain words on the search screen (distance, availability, specialty match, verification, the doctor's reliability, such as joining online consultations on time, and, later, ratings). **[CD-05; E-Commerce Rules: explain the main ranking parameters. - We do not sell medicines in this version.
- Reminders and other app features help you follow your doctor's instructions. They are not medical advice. **[Intended-use statements, S-022]**

## 6. Online consultations

- Online consultations follow the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020.
- **Online consultations are only available while you are in India.** You confirm this when you book and before you join. If you are abroad, you can book a clinic visit for when you are back. **[Guidelines exclude consultations outside India; VALIDATE]**
- **Consent:** by booking an online consultation, you agree to be consulted online. If a doctor or clinic starts the consultation, you will be asked to agree.
- **Identity:** the doctor will confirm your name and age, and the doctor's name, qualifications and registration number are shown to you.
- **Limits:** the doctor may decide that you need to be seen in person. Some medicines cannot be prescribed online, and some need a video consultation. The doctor may decline to prescribe them.
- **No recording:** we do not record video or audio consultations.
- Join from a quiet, private place with a working connection. If the doctor does not join, you get an automatic refund.
