# Review Policy — [Product Name] (applies from v1.1, when reviews launch)

**Version:** REV-1.0-draft · **Status:** DRAFT for lawyer review. Follows CD-08 in [doctor-registration-and-conduct.md](../clinical/doctor-registration-and-conduct.md), the E-Commerce Rules 2020, the Consumer Protection dark-patterns and misleading-ads guidelines, the voluntary IS 19000:2022 standard on online reviews, and the NMC guidelines of 6 Oct 2026 (ban on fake or manipulated reviews). **[VALIDATE]**

## Who can review

- Only a patient whose appointment was **completed** on [Product Name], within 14 days of the visit. One review per visit.
- Reviews show your first name and initial, or "Verified patient", if you choose. We never show your phone number or health details.

## What a review can say

- Your own experience: waiting time, how the doctor explained things, the clinic, booking and payment.
- **Do not include** health details about yourself or anyone else, names of other patients or staff, phone numbers, abuse, threats, discrimination, adverts or links, or anything false.
- Reviews are about the experience, not medical advice to others.

## What we never do

- We never pay for reviews or reward them, and we never let doctors or clinics do so.
- We ask **every** patient with a completed visit for a review, the same way, not only the ones likely to be happy.
- We never hide, delay or edit genuine negative reviews, and we never let a doctor pay to change ratings or ranking.
- We do not use patient reviews in advertising.

## How ratings are shown

- The rating is an adjusted average, so a few reviews cannot dominate (S-214). The number of reviews is always shown.
- **Ratings never depend on payment** (CD-05).

## Moderation

- Reviews are checked before they are published (automatic checks plus a person) for the rules above.
- A review that breaks the rules is removed or returned to the writer to edit, and **the reason is logged** (S-213).
- The doctor or clinic can post **one public reply** and can report a review. Reporting does not hide the review while we check it.
- Content reported as unlawful is dealt with within 36 hours (IT Rules, as amended in Feb 2026).

## Disagreements

If your review was removed and you disagree, contact the Grievance Officer (see the [Grievance Redressal](grievance-redressal.md) page). We reply within 24 hours and aim to resolve within 7 days.
