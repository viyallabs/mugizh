## If you are not satisfied

- **Data protection:** the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Content decisions by us as an intermediary:** the Grievance Appellate Committee (gac.gov.in), within 30 days of our decision. **[VALIDATE]**
- **Consumer complaints:** the National Consumer Helpline (1915 or consumerhelpline.gov.in), or the consumer commission for your district.
- **A doctor's professional conduct:** we pass serious complaints to the clinic and record them for re-checking the doctor's registration. You can also complain to the State Medical Council.
